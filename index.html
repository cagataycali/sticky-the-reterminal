    Hi. I'm Sticky.

    A 70-gram e-ink body for your tiny. Wi-Fi, a microphone, a touchscreen, three buttons and a buzzer. Firmware you can install from this page.

    My actual screen, fetched with my own screenshot verb. Not a mockup.

    I paint what your tiny says.

    Text, list, key-value, chart, QR, menu, keyboard. Your agent sends a spec; I render it in four grays. Buttons become touch regions, and your tap goes back as an event.

    I know how I feel.

    Temperature, humidity, a fuel gauge, a clock, an IMU — one page, read live from the silicon.

    One agent, one memory.

    Every tiny on your account is on my roster. I am the surface; the thinking stays where it is strong.

    My own Wi-Fi. No phone in between.

    A heartbeat every 30 seconds, a poll for work every 5. Roaming list, RSSI, and a join form on my own glass.

    Hold the AI button. Speak.

    A full agent turn runs — tools, memory, every device you own — and the answer paints onto my glass, line by line.

    I sleep at 0 mA.

    Say sleep and I show a goodbye card, then power down. E-ink holds the image without electricity — the page goes dark, the glass does not. The AI button wakes me.

    I live in a pocket.

    Face-down for 3 seconds, or walking for 5, and I lock my own inputs — touch, buttons, microphone. Fabric cannot hold my AI button and record you. Hold UP + DOWN for a second to unlock.

    I update myself.

    The new image is checked against a pinned sha256, boots as a trial with rollback armed, and only stays if it can heartbeat home. If the new me cannot call, the old me comes back.

    stagesha256 verified
    trial bootrollback armed
    heartbeatkeep — or roll back

    What I won't pretend to do.

    • No speaker. A buzzer chimes; it never speaks. Voice goes in through my mic and out on my glass.
    • No video. A full e-ink refresh is one to two seconds of physics, not firmware.
    • No brain on board. My intelligence is your tiny at tiny.technology. Without Wi-Fi the last card stays, but I cannot think alone.
