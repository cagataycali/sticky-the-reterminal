    Sound-site audit — every beep in the firmware, and whether per-event mutes are warranted·

    [builder] 2026-08-27, fw 0.25.29. Method: grep every caller of sticky_buzzer_beep, sticky_buzzer_beep_double, tiny_audio_chime; read each site in context. This audit exists because the sounds section's next planned row was "per-event toggles" — and an audit is cheaper than a settings matrix nobody asked for.

    Inventory (23 call sites, 5 semantic classes)·

    1. Touch/button feedback — the 3-word vocabulary (15 sites)·

    tiny_button.cpp (5: refuse-double on dead ring ends, pushed page, error chime on action fail), tiny_touch.cpp (1: accept), tiny_display.cpp (8: gesture accepts, stream-owns-display refusal, keyboard-guard refusal, page turns "sound on turn"), tiny_shell.cpp silent-row demo (1). These are UX_SPEC §0's words: single = accepted, double = recognized-but-refused. On e-ink the sound IS the explanation — a silent refusal reads as a broken panel.

    2. Lock transitions (2 sites)·

    tiny_lock.cpp: double-blip on lock ("inputs are now refused", audible through a pocket — P-1's safety readback), single on unlock.

    3. Ask/voice ceremony (2 sites)·

    tiny_node.cpp:478,490: chime("ask") when mic capture starts, chime("ack") when the recording is accepted for upload.

    4. Notification (1 site)·

    tiny_node.cpp:1719: incoming pushed card — "a new card deserves a sound". The only unsolicited sound in the firmware.

    5. Boot chime (1 site)·

    tiny_main.cpp:75: audible "I'm alive" (skipped on glance wake).

    Plus 2 self-referential settings demos (silent row unmute-beep, beepvol tier demo) — these ARE their own toggle, not candidates.

    Ruling: per-event toggle matrix — NOT WARRANTED·

    • Classes 1, 2 are feedback, not noise: they answer a touch the human just made, at a moment their hand is on the device. Muting refuse-doubles separately would revive the "broken panel" reading the vocabulary exists to prevent. Anyone who wants them gone wants silent, which exists (owner P0) and now has a volume tier below it.
    • Class 3 is a privacy affordance: the ask-chime marks the mic going hot. A separate mute for exactly this sound is an anti-feature.
    • Class 5 fires once per cold boot and already respects silent.
    • Class 4 — the notification chirp — is the ONLY class with a real independent-mute story ("I want touch feedback but not unsolicited chirps"). One row, not a matrix: if the reviewer or owner wants it, it is notif chirp: ON/OFF next to the beeps row, NVS u8, gating only tiny_node:1719. Filed as OPTIONAL — not built until someone asks, per the glance-toggle precedent (a control nobody needs is clutter with a border).

    Question filed (owner/reviewer)·

    Should the ask-chime (class 3) be EXEMPT from silent mode? Silent currently mutes the sound that announces the mic is recording. The counter-argument: the listening frame on the glass also announces it, visibly, and an owner who chose silent chose silent. We change nothing without a ruling; noted because a privacy signal that can be muted is a decision someone should make on purpose, once, in writing.

    Sounds section verdict·

    Volume tier ✓ (0.25.29) + silent ✓ (P0) = the section as designed is complete. Optional notif chirp row parked behind an explicit ask.
