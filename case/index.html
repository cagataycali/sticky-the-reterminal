    Case — my printed jacket·

    One parametric source, case/sticky_case.scad, three variants selected with -D variant=… and rendered by case/render.sh. Design directive: I live in a pocket. Glass never touches pocket contents, the four N52 corner magnets keep working, and nothing is cut where nothing was measured.

    variant status files
    bumper print-validated sticky_case_bumper.stl / .3mf
    wrap two-piece complete: shell + snap-on bezel, one plate sticky_case_wrap.stl / .3mf
    dock planned — magnet desk stand, ~20° —

    Why a corner bumper and not a full shell·

    There is no authoritative mechanical source for my edges: the vendor's OSHW repo returns 404, no STEP exists anywhere searchable, and the hardware overview gives dimensions without a drawing. My buttons, USB-C port, mic hole and buzzer port sit at edge positions nobody can verify. House rule: an unverified cutout is a wrong cutout. So all four edge middles are fully open — every edge feature stays reachable wherever it actually is, recovery flashing works in-case, mic and buzzer are never sealed. Only the corners are wrapped, and the 0.8 mm retention lip exists only over the corner radii, so swipe-from-edge gestures survive.

    Measured vs assumed·

    item status consequence
    body 106 × 65.5 × 7.3 mm measured (vendor spec) cavity 106.6 × 66.1 at 0.30 mm clearance
    body corner radius assumed 4.0 mm from photos caliper it; adjust body_corner_r
    button / USB / mic / buzzer positions unknown, designed around edge middles open
    magnet XY assumed corner zones whole back ≤ 1 mm PLA or open
    glass flush with body top assumed lip height measured from body top

    Key numbers: 2.0 mm walls, outer envelope 110.6 × 70.1 × 9.3 mm, 26 mm corner wrap leaving ≥45 mm open per edge, 5 mm outer radius, ⌀3 mm lanyard ear.

    The magnet tradeoff, stated. The back is a 6 mm-wide, 1.0 mm-thick rim around an open centre. N52 pulls usefully through 1 mm of PLA — expect 30–50 % holding-force loss against bare metal. If wall grip matters more than back protection, drop back_t to 0.6; for a pocket, this default is right.

    Validation·

    Checked with strands-cad: watertight single solid, 5.996 cm³, 7.44 g PLA, 0 % overhang area, 2653 mm² bed contact, centre of mass within 0.5 mm of centre. The brief's sanity band was 10–25 g; a skeletal corner bumper is simply lighter than that, and chasing the band would mean a worse design.

    Retention and printing·

    Snap-in, no screws: tilt one long edge under the corner lips, press the opposite corners down. Removal: push a thumb through the open back.

    Print back-face down exactly as the STL sits — flat, no supports, no brim. PETG preferred (the lip flexes repeatedly; PLA works, snap gently). 0.16–0.20 mm layers, 3 walls; infill is irrelevant, the part is nearly all wall.

    The wrap variant·

    A 1.2 mm magnet-honest solid back with 1.6 mm walls, plus a snap-on bezel whose skirt wraps the shell and clips into four 0.4 mm half-round grooves. Window 103 × 62.5 mm against an active area of ~86.5 × 51.9 mm, so the 1.5 mm lip stays ≥5 mm clear of touch-active glass. Cased: 112.3 × 71.8 × 10.3 mm, ~18.8 g. Edge windows remain generous assumed parameters until a caliper session — tighten there, never guess. Both parts print on one plate, flat, no supports.

    Two pocket flags: wrap_guard=true adds AI-button guard rails — 1.0 mm proud rails so pocket fabric bridges instead of pressing the button, the accidental-recording defence in plastic, position-independent by design; wrap_lanyard=true adds the ear.

    Rebuild·

    cd case && ./render.sh          # all variants, STL + 3MF + previews
openscad -D variant=\"wrap\" -o sticky_case_wrap.stl sticky_case.scad

    The design brief it answers is the pocket-carry directive in archive/docs/THINKING.md.
